In the modern enterprise, “the edge” is rarely a sleek, purpose-built facility. Instead, mission-critical workloads are increasingly housed in repurposed storage rooms, telecom closets, and back offices. As edge adoption accelerates, these “closet edge” environments have become central to IT strategy.
